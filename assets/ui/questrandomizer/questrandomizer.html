Questrandomizer

Before every re-roll / re-play, a backup of the profile and its quest data is saved to:
\SPT\user\mods\Leaves-Questrandomizer\backups\
Select accepted quests and re-roll their randomization. Progress on a re-rolled quest is reset. Applies after a client restart.
Or use Re-roll locked to refresh every quest you haven't accepted, completed, or failed — handy after a config update. Nothing in progress is touched, no progress is reset.
⚠ Admin mode re-rolls quests the config deliberately protects (blacklisted or non-whitelisted vanilla quests). A scripted or fragile quest may become harder to complete or break its flow. Use at your own risk.
